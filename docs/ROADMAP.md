# Empirical Roadmap

The manuscript proposes a staged program.

## Stage 0 — v1.0 replication kit
Reproduce the current deterministic microimplementation and verify the published estimands.

## Stage 1 — Extended simulation
- 2D or lightweight 3D environment;
- all five fault sites;
- held-out split/merge rules;
- at least two agent architectures;
- at least three generator families;
- pilot, power simulation, and preregistration.

## Stage 2 — Targeted and factorial ablations
Test shared-interface ablations, discriminant validity, and compare conjunctive, scalar, Pareto, and simpler baselines.

## Stage 3 — Morphology and adversarial perception
Introduce tool incorporation, cross-sensor inconsistency, correlated failures, morphology changes, and concurrent-fault extensions.

## Stage 4 — Physical replication
Port a constrained version to a tabletop robot with camera, proprioception, and force/tactile sensing; test under physical noise, latency, wear, and calibration drift.

## Stage 5 — Separate consciousness analysis
Only if warranted by robust functional results. Do not retrofit the autonomy benchmark to imply consciousness.

## Current boundary

This v1.0 kit implements **Stage 0 only**.
